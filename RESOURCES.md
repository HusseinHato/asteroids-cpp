# C++ and SFML resources

## Knowledge
- [SFML 2.6.2 Drawable](https://www.sfml-dev.org/documentation/2.6.2/classsf_1_1Drawable.html): the drawing override used by Ship and Bullet. Also available in the fetched SFML headers under build/_deps/sfml-src/include.
- [SFML 2.6.2 CircleShape](https://www.sfml-dev.org/documentation/2.6.2/classsf_1_1CircleShape.html): radius and shape methods for the bullet.
- [SFML 2.6.2 Transformable](https://www.sfml-dev.org/documentation/2.6.2/classsf_1_1Transformable.html): position, origin, and rotation.
- [Microsoft C++ constructors](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/cpp/constructors-cpp?view=msvc-170): constructor syntax and member initialization.
- [Microsoft vector reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/standard-library/vector-class?view=msvc-170): collection ownership, insertion, erasure, and invalidated references for lesson 3.
